Cancellation & Refund Policy

What happens if your plans change, and what happens if ours do.

Last updated: August 2026. This policy applies to all bookings made through rasnaexperience.com, both fixed-date departures and custom-date trips.

Rasna trips are small, all-inclusive, and booked for a specific set of dates. Because we commit to accommodation and local hosts on your behalf as soon as you book — often with our own non-refundable deposits — we can't offer a blanket "cancel anytime for a full refund" policy. This page sets out exactly what you get back, and when, if your plans change.

Your right to cancel

Under EU consumer law, the standard 14-day right of withdrawal for online purchases does not apply to bookings like this one — accommodation and leisure services booked for a specific date are excluded from that right under Article 16(l) of EU Directive 2011/83/EU. Instead, the terms below apply from the moment your booking is confirmed.

The 20% booking fee reflects deposits and commitments we make to accommodation and local hosts on your behalf as soon as you book, which we can't recover. We strongly recommend travel insurance that covers trip cancellation for your own peace of mind — it typically covers more situations (illness, work emergencies, etc.) than we're able to.

Unavoidable and extraordinary circumstances

If unavoidable and extraordinary circumstances at or near Blera — natural disasters, war, government travel restrictions, or similar events significantly affecting the trip — make it impossible or unsafe to go ahead, you're entitled to a full refund regardless of the timing above. This does not cover personal circumstances (illness, a change of plans, missed flights) — that's what travel insurance is for.

If we cancel your departure

If Rasna cancels your trip for any reason within our control, you'll receive a full refund within 14 days, or the option to rebook a future departure if you'd prefer.

Because groups are intentionally small, a fixed departure may occasionally need a minimum number of guests to run. If we need to cancel a departure for this reason, we'll tell you no later than 20 days before the trip starts, and you'll receive a full refund or the option to move to another date.

If we have to cancel due to unavoidable and extraordinary circumstances (see above), you'll still receive a full refund of everything you've paid — we just won't owe additional compensation beyond that.

Governing law

This policy is governed by Italian law, without prejudice to any mandatory consumer-protection rights you're entitled to under the law of your own country of residence.
