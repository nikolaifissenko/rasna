## The Cantine Festival: when it actually is

The festival's real name is **"Per San Martino ogni mosto diventa vino"**
(Festa del Vino e dell'Olio Novello nelle Cantine), organised by the Pro
Loco di Blera. It is tied to San Martino (Nov 11), and the docs' "second
weekend of November" is only roughly right. Past editions, all Fri–Sun:

| Year | Dates | Nov 1 fell on |
|---|---|---|
| 2011 | Nov 11–13 | Tue |
| 2014 | Nov 7–9 | Sat |
| 2015 | Nov 6–8 | Sun |
| 2023 | Nov 10–12 | Wed |
| 2024 | Nov 8–10 | Fri |
| 2025 | Nov 7–9 (XVI edition) | Sat |

**Rule that fits every year: the weekend ending on the second Sunday of
November.**

- **2027: Fri Nov 12 – Sun Nov 14.** Every rule agrees, so the date is
  safe to plan around (still confirm with the Pro Loco once they announce,
  usually October: prolocodiblera@gmail.com).
- **2026 warning:** Nov 1 2026 is a Sunday, the same calendar as 2015,
  when the festival ran **Nov 6–8**. If 2026 follows 2015, it is the
  weekend *before* the Nov 9–15 departure, and the live site's promise of
  "Cantine Festival, Sat Nov 14" is wrong. No 2026 date was published as
  of 2026-10-10. Confirm with the Pro Loco before anyone books.

## Day by day

### Monday, November 8
- Airport pick up (FCO) and transfer to Blera
- Welcome aperitivo in the piazza
- Dinner at local trattoria

### Tuesday, November 9
- Breakfast
- Tomb raiding scavenger hunt: starts in the medieval village, follows clues down into the rock-cut tombs, host-guided
- Lunch
- Tarquinia day trip: Etruscan necropolis (2026 price €13/person)
- Dinner in Tarquinia

### Wednesday, November 10
- Italian farmer's breakfast
- Olive harvest in a grove kept unpicked for the group (see risks)
- Farmer's lunch: focaccia panini with local cold cuts, cheeses and sauces
- Afternoon: Tuscia Terme (2026 quote €14/person)
- Home-cooked local dinner

### Thursday, November 11 (San Martino)
- Breakfast at the bar
- Morning at the frantoio to collect the oil pressed from Wednesday's harvest
- Lunch at Terrarte, in the olive grove among the sculptures
- Afternoon: pasta making
- Evening: San Martino itself, roast chestnuts and the first new wine of the year

### Friday, November 12 (festival opens)
- Breakfast
- Hike: San Giovenale
- Panonto BBQ lunch (bread roasted over embers, new oil, grilled meats; 2026 estimate ~€25–27/guest)
- Dinner: Cantina Il Cavone as the festival opens (2026 quote €25/person)

### Saturday, November 13 (festival)
- Breakfast, plus hangover cure
- Free day: the cellars open for lunch and dinner
- Goodbye dinner in a festival cantina

### Sunday, November 14
- Last breakfast, last festival lunch for anyone on a late flight
- Goodbyes, FCO transfer

## What changes from 2026
- One day earlier in the calendar (Mon 8 to Sun 14), so the festival lands on Fri–Sat as the site already describes. All weekday activities stay on the same weekday.
- Thursday now falls on San Martino itself: worth a small moment in the evening.
- The Saturday goodbye dinner moves *into* the festival (a cellar booked for the group) instead of a separate venue still unnamed in the 2026 plan.

## Risks to lock in writing
- **Early harvest.** 2026's harvest started in September, earlier than usual. Before selling, get in writing: one grove left unpicked for Nov 10, and a frantoio still pressing on Nov 11.
- **Festival cantine are run by town associations**, not restaurants. A group of 8 needs a table booked through the Pro Loco, not just a walk-in.
- All 2026 prices above need fresh 2027 quotes.
