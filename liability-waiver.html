Liability Waiver & Assumption of Risk

Our trips are hands-on and physical. Here's what that means, and what we ask of you in return.

Last updated: August 2026. This applies to every guest who books a Rasna trip through rasnaexperience.com. By booking, you confirm you've read and accept it.

Rasna is not a bus tour. Guests harvest olives, descend into 2,800-year-old rock-cut tombs, ride horses through the countryside, forage in the woods, and work in kitchens with knives, fire, and hot equipment. Those experiences are the point — but like any real, physical, outdoor activity, they carry real risk. This page explains that risk plainly, so you can decide, informed, whether the trip is right for you.

Nature of the activities

Depending on which activities you choose, your trip may include:

  • Walking on uneven, ancient terrain — rock-cut Etruscan tombs and the Via Clodia involve steps, narrow passages, low clearances, loose stone, and unguarded drops.
  • Horseback riding at Civitella Cesi — an inherently unpredictable activity involving a live animal, regardless of your experience level or the animal's temperament.
  • Farm and harvest work — olive, hazelnut, and tomato harvesting involve ladders, hand tools, uneven orchard and field ground, and occasional exposure to insects, sun, and weather.
  • Foraging for wild asparagus and other seasonal ingredients in woodland, off maintained paths.
  • Hands-on cooking and food production — knives, open flame, hot oil, wood-fired ovens, and curing/butchery demonstrations with Emiliano and Davide.
  • Local transport on rural Italian roads between Blera, Civitella Cesi, and day-trip destinations.

Local hosts — Maria Grazia, Davide, Emiliano, Renzo, and others — run some of these activities as independent providers. Rasna selects and coordinates them, but doesn't control their day-to-day operation of an animal, kitchen, or workshop.

Assumption of risk

By booking, you acknowledge that these activities carry inherent risks — including slips, falls, animal-related injury, cuts, burns, insect stings, allergic reactions, and motion-related injury — that cannot be eliminated even with reasonable care, and you voluntarily choose to take part with knowledge of those risks.

This doesn't ask you to sign away rights the law doesn't let us take. Under Italian law, we can't and don't exclude our liability for death or personal injury caused by our own negligence, gross negligence, or wilful misconduct, or for any liability that can't lawfully be limited (Italian Civil Code, Art. 1229). What it does mean is that Rasna is not liable for injury or loss arising from the ordinary, inherent risks of these activities when we've taken reasonable care — the kind of risk that comes with riding a horse or walking down a 2,800-year-old staircase, not from something we did wrong.

What we ask of you

  • Come reasonably fit for a physically active week — walking, some uneven ground, and a full day's pace most days.
  • Tell us about any medical condition, mobility limitation, allergy, or pregnancy that might affect your participation, before you book if possible.
  • Follow the instructions of your guide, host, or activity leader, particularly around horses, tools, knives, and tomb access — and sit out any activity you're not comfortable with. You're always free to opt out on the day.
  • Wear appropriate footwear and clothing for each activity (see our packing guidance).
  • Arrange travel insurance that covers activity-related injury, medical treatment, and evacuation in Italy. This is your responsibility, not something we provide.

Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by Italian and EU law, Rasna's liability for any claim relating to your trip is limited to direct damages arising from our own proven negligence, and excludes indirect or consequential loss (such as missed connecting travel or lost income). Nothing here limits any right you have under mandatory consumer-protection law, including for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, or for any of our local hosts acting on our behalf.

Governing law

This waiver is governed by Italian law, without prejudice to any mandatory consumer-protection rights you're entitled to under the law of your own country of residence. See also our Cancellation & Refund Policy.
