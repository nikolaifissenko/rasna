# Rasna — Contingency & Backup Plans

What to do if a planned activity for the November 9–15, 2026 pilot
departure can't happen as scheduled: bad weather, a host unavailable,
a venue closed, or a booking falling through. Companion to
`ITINERARY_NOV2026.md` (the plan as it should go) and `CONTATTI_LOCALI.md`
(who everyone is). Doesn't cover guest injury/medical response
(`liability-waiver.html` §"Emergency medical treatment") or
refund terms if Rasna itself has to cancel the whole departure
(`cancellation-policy.html`) — this is specifically about keeping
*this week* running when one piece of it falls through.

## Guiding principles

1. **Saturday is already a built-in buffer.** It's a free day plus the
   Cantine Festival — lowest-fixed-commitment day of the week. If a
   weather-exposed activity earlier in the week gets rained out, the
   first move is to try to slot it into Saturday before improvising
   something new, since it means zero new bookings/costs.
2. **Decide the day before, not the morning of, where possible.** Check
   the forecast the evening before an outdoor day (Wed harvest, Fri
   hike/panonto, Tue tomb walk) and call the host then — gives them a
   chance to adjust and gives guests a heads-up at breakfast instead of
   a surprise change mid-morning.
3. **Never leave a day empty.** Every swap below aims for
   activity-for-activity, not activity-for-nothing — guests paid for a
   full week.
4. **Tell guests plainly, don't oversell the swap.** "Weather made X
   not workable today, so we're doing Y instead" lands fine; guests on
   this kind of trip are explicitly not expecting a scripted tour.
