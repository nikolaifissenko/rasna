Why book with me

Nikolai guides full-time in Rome, but Blera is different. He grew up here, literally in the ruins. He leads every departure himself, capped at 8 guests, because it's the community that raised him, not a script for a bus load.

His family's house sits in the village, rebuilt stone by stone by his father, and the Etruscan tombs that ring it were his actual childhood playground. He was leading relatives through them before he knew the word "guide."

English runs the trip, but Nikolai also speaks Italian, French, Spanish, and Russian fluently, so if it's easier to ask a question or tell a story in another language, use it.
