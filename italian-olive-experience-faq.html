Before You Book

Everything guests ask before reserving their spot.

What is the Italian Olive Experience?

It's Rasna's fixed-departure week (November 9–15, 2026) in Blera, an ancient Etruscan hill town in Italy's Tuscia region. Guests harvest olives, explore rock-cut Etruscan tombs, visit thermal baths, and join the town's Festa delle Cantine wine festival, all as a small group capped at 8 guests.

How is this different from a normal Italy tour?

Most Italy tours move large groups through sights by bus. Rasna caps every trip at 8 guests and builds it around real local life: you work alongside the people who actually farm the land, cure the meat, and cook the meals, rather than watching a staged demonstration.

What's included in the price?

€1,825 per person if you book by September 15, 2026, €2,125 per person after that, all-inclusive: accommodation, all meals, guided Etruscan site visits, the olive harvest and Cantine Festival, and local transport. The only extra costs are flights, travel insurance, and personal spending. See the full pricing chart.

Is there a discount for booking early?

Yes. Book by September 15, 2026 and it's €1,825 per person. After that it's €2,125 per person, right up until departure. See the full pricing chart.

Can I also build my own custom trip instead?

Yes. Alongside the fixed Italian Olive Experience departure, Rasna offers fully custom small-group Italy experiences. Pick your own activities and dates and Rasna arranges everything around you.

Where is Blera?

Blera is in Tuscia, the historic Etruscan region of northern Lazio, about 90 minutes from Rome. Far enough to avoid the Tuscany and Amalfi Coast tourist crowds, close enough for an easy transfer.

What should I pack?

Layers. Mornings and evenings are cool, afternoons can still be mild. Comfortable walking shoes (the tombs and San Giovenale are uneven ground), a light rain jacket, something a little nicer for the Festa delle Cantine, and a swimsuit for Tuscia Terme's thermal baths.

Do I need to speak Italian?

No. English runs the trip. Nikolai also speaks Italian, French, Spanish, and Russian, so if it's easier to ask a question or tell a story in another language, use it.

What's the cancellation policy?

Cancel 30+ days before departure for a full refund, 15–29 days for 50%, and it's non-refundable within 14 days, since accommodation and local partners are booked and paid on your behalf in advance. Unavoidable circumstances at or near Blera, or a cancellation by us, get a full refund regardless of timing. See the full policy.

Is this trip safe? Who's liable if I get hurt?

The trip is hands-on and physical — hiking, horseback or donkey riding, foraging, farm work, and similar activities carry inherent risk, like any real outdoor activity. You're responsible for your own safety and for following your guide's instructions; if you're hurt through the ordinary risk of an activity and not through our fault, that's not on Rasna. If we (or a local host) are at fault, Italian and EU package-travel law means that liability can't be waived away. See our full Liability Waiver & Assumption of Risk.
