## Why Easter week
- **Anchor event:** Blera's Pasquetta pilgrimage. On Easter Monday the town
  walks from the church of Santa Maria (meeting 7:30) to the grotto of San
  Vivenzio. Like the Cantine Festival in November, it is something the town
  does for itself, and guests are simply there for it. Easter 2027 is
  **Sunday March 28**, so Pasquetta is **Monday March 29**.
- **Seasonal work that is real in late March:** wild asparagus foraging
  (March–June, a living Easter tradition) and Carciofo Romanesco artichokes
  (January–May).
- **The Easter table:** the biggest family meal of the spring. The most
  direct way to sell belonging, not sightseeing.

Shape: Tuesday to Monday instead of Monday to Sunday, so the trip ends on
the pilgrimage.

## Risks
- **Easter is a family holiday.** Hosts, restaurants and drivers may not work on Easter Sunday or Pasquetta. Confirm the Sunday lunch and the Monday transfer first; if they can't be locked, the dates don't work.
- **Pasquetta traffic** on the roads back to Rome: leave a big margin for evening flights, or book guests on Tuesday flights with an optional extra night.
- **Weather:** late March can be cold and wet. Keep the Terme day movable (see `CONTINGENCY_PLAN.md` logic).
- **Asparagus** depends on the rain in February and March. Thin year: fall back to artichokes.
- **Good Friday** is traditionally meatless for many families; the cheese part of the Civitella Cesi day is fine, keep any cured-meat tasting off Friday.
