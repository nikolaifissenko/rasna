## Metrics to watch (first 2 weeks)

- **Saves** on Post 5 (checklist format) — the clearest signal the content is useful, not just liked
- **Profile visits → link clicks** on Posts 2 and 6 — the actual funnel to a booking
- **Reel completion rate** on Post 1 — if it drops off before 8s, the hook isn't landing and needs a rewrite, not a new posting time

## Algorithm tip

Post the Reel (Post 1) first and let it run 48 hours before posting anything else — Instagram tests new accounts' Reels against a small audience first, and a second post too soon can split that early signal. Everything after Post 1 can follow the 2–3 day cadence above.
