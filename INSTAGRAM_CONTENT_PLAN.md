# Instagram Content Plan — Assets, Profile, Reels, Launch Calendar

Companion to `INSTAGRAM_STRATEGY.md` (which covers voice, pillars, hashtags,
posting cadence). This doc is the concrete production plan: what to shoot,
what's already made, what the profile should look like, and what to post in
the first 4 weeks. Written 2026-08-08.

## 0. The honest starting point

I audited the whole repo and the live site for anything postable. Result:
**one real image asset exists** — `assets/og-image.png`, a branded text
card, not a photo. No trip photos, no photos of Maria Grazia/Davide/Emiliano,
no photos of Blera, the tombs, or the olive groves exist anywhere in this
project. Zero pilot groups have run yet (`BUSINESS_PLAN.md` Phase 2 is not
started), so there's no guest content either.

This isn't a blocker — it's actually consistent with the brand's own pitch.
`INSTAGRAM_STRATEGY.md` already says "unpolished beats staged" and Instagram's
2025 algorithm rewards native, un-produced video over polish (see
`references/strategie-croissance.md` in the smm-pro skill — Reels completion
rate and shares beat everything, and stock-photo-looking content reads as
inauthentic for exactly this niche). So the plan below leans into that: a
**founder-shot documentary account**, not a brochure. What's missing is not
a photographer — it's Nikolai's phone, in Blera, pointed at real things.

## 1. Profile picture — decided

Generated via Canva to match the site's existing Etruscan/terracotta
identity (same palette as `og-image.png`). Final pick and one backup are in
`assets/instagram/`:

- **`profile-picture-amphora.png`** (primary) — a gold Etruscan amphora
  inside a weathered Greek-key ring on dark terracotta. Reads clearly at
  Instagram's tiny avatar size because the amphora is a solid filled shape,
  not thin linework.
- **`profile-picture-bold-backup.png`** — a flatter, higher-contrast
  version of the same mark, if the textured one looks muddy once
  compressed by Instagram.

**Why an emblem, not a photo or the wordmark:** no founder headshot or
usable photo exists yet to make a personal profile picture credible, and
the full "RASNA" wordmark (as used in `og-image.png`) turns into an
unreadable smear at 32–110px — text-heavy marks never survive Instagram's
avatar crop. A simple, recognizable icon does. Revisit this once there's a
strong candidate photo (e.g., a hero shot from the pilot) — but the amphora
mark is a fine long-term choice too; plenty of travel/hospitality brands
keep an emblem permanently rather than switching to a face.

**To set it:** open the Instagram app → Edit Profile → Change Profile
Photo → upload `profile-picture-amphora.png` from your phone (AirDrop/email
it to yourself, or I can hand it to you directly — see attached). Should
take under a minute; this isn't something I can push remotely since it's an
in-app action tied to your login.

## 3. Shot list — what to actually go capture

Ranked by urgency. Everything here uses real people already confirmed in
`CONTATTI_LOCALI.md` — no new outreach needed to start shooting.

### This month (August) — do this first, it's time-sensitive
**Tomato / sauce day (passata)** is confirmed available *right now* per
`BUSINESS_PLAN.md` §1 ("Maria Grazia is confident this can happen even if
she has to plant the tomatoes herself"). This is the single best shoot
opportunity on the calendar — go before the window closes:
- Hands crushing/processing tomatoes, close-up, no faces needed if anyone's
  camera-shy
- Jars being filled and sealed — satisfying, saveable Reel material
  ("sauce day" ASMR-style clip)
- Maria Grazia narrating in Italian with English captions — authenticity >
  polish, subtitles do the work
- Wide shot of the outdoor cooking setup (wood fire, big pots) — this is
  the single most "you can't get this anywhere else" visual on the list

### Anytime — artisan visits (year-round, no seasonal dependency)
- **Davide Paolocci, Civitella Cesi** — cheese/meat tasting + hands-on
  production. Confirmed, available now. Shoot: the aging cellar/storage,
  hands working the cheese, the tasting spread, the nearby archaeological
  site he includes.
- **Emiliano (Miriam's son) — affettati** — confirmed, available now.
  Shoot: the charcuterie itself (this is straightforwardly gorgeous food
  content on its own), the degustation table, Emiliano talking about the
  family recipe.
- **Renzo — leatherwork** — high availability, no hosting fee needed since
  guests buy afterward. Shoot: hands cutting/stitching leather, tools,
  finished bags — this is exactly the kind of "watch a real craftsman"
  process content that performs well as a Reel (per
  `references/contenu-copy.md`'s hook templates for artisan/process
  content).

### The place (no people needed — solo shoot, any time)
- Blera's rock-cut Etruscan tombs and the Via Clodia — golden hour
  (early morning or last 90 min before sunset)
- The hill town itself — narrow streets, the view out over the valley
- Olive groves before harvest (establishing shots now; harvest footage
  comes Oct–Dec)

### October–December — olive harvest (the other reliable seasonal anchor)
Same shot list style as sauce day: hands on the trees, the press, the oil
coming out, communal meal afterward. This is also when the booked Nov 9–15
departure happens — see §5.

### Minimum viable capture session
If Nikolai only has one afternoon free this month: Maria Grazia + sauce day
+ 10 minutes at the tombs on the way out. That alone is 2–3 weeks of Reels
and posts once edited down.

## 4. Reels lineup — 10 concrete concepts, scripted

Each follows the HOOK-VALEUR-CTA structure from `references/contenu-copy.md`.
7–15 seconds is the target length; subtitles mandatory (80% watch muted).

1. **"The tomato sauce nobody sells you"** (sauce day, this month)
   - Hook (0-2s): text on screen over hands in tomatoes — "This is where
     your pasta sauce actually comes from"
   - Value (2-12s): fast cuts — crushing, cooking, jarring, sealing
   - CTA: "We built a trip around days like this. Link in bio."

2. **"Nobody told me Italy still had unmapped tombs"**
   - Hook: walking shot approaching a rock-cut tomb entrance, text: "90
     minutes from Rome, 2,800 years old, and almost no tourists"
   - Value: quick interior/exterior shots, one fact on screen
   - CTA: "Full guide linked in bio" (points to
     `etruscan-tombs-guide.html`)

3. **"Meet Davide"** (people pillar)
   - Hook: Davide mid-cheesemaking, no intro — "This is the only place in
     Italy you'll learn this recipe"
   - Value: 3 quick clips of the process, his voice (subtitled)
   - CTA: "Comment BLERA and we'll send the full guide"

4. **"What €1,450 actually gets you"** (practical/FAQ pillar — pairs with
   the carousel in §2)
   - Hook: text-on-screen, no video needed if nothing's shot yet, or cut
     over b-roll: "Here's exactly what's included"
   - Value: 5 bullet points on screen (lodging, all meals, every activity,
     transport, no hidden extras)
   - CTA: "2 founding-guest spots left at 15% off — link in bio"
     (ties directly to the live discount in `BOOKING_STATUS.md`)

5. **"The leather bag Americans keep asking for"** (Renzo)
   - Hook: hands cutting leather — "Every American guest we've run in Rome
     asks the same question"
   - Value: quick process shots, finished bag
   - CTA: "You make your own. Link in bio."

6. **"Why we cap every group at 10 people"**
   - Hook: talking head (Nikolai, phone selfie is fine) — "Here's the one
     rule we never break"
   - Value: explain the bus-tour contrast in 2 sentences
   - CTA: "See the next departure — link in bio"

7. **"A day that doesn't exist on a bus tour"** (day-in-the-life format,
   once the first real day is shot — sauce day or an artisan visit)
   - Hook: "7am, and we're already behind schedule" (relatable, humble)
   - Value: quick montage across the day
   - CTA: "Follow along — we're building this in public"

8. **"Building Rasna" behind-the-scenes** (process/authenticity pillar,
   shootable today, zero dependencies)
   - Hook: Nikolai on camera — "Most travel companies fake the 'authentic
     Italy' thing. We're trying to actually do it."
   - Value: quick cuts of outreach calls, the site, a WhatsApp with Maria
     Grazia
   - CTA: "Watch us build it — new posts weekly"

9. **"Etruscan, not Roman"** (educational/place pillar, no shoot needed —
   can be built entirely from stock knowledge + text-on-screen over tomb or
   countryside b-roll once available, or a talking-head explainer meanwhile)
   - Hook: "Everyone assumes Tuscia is Roman. It's older than Rome."
   - Value: 3 fast facts
   - CTA: "Full history in the guide — link in bio"

10. **Guest reaction / testimonial** (once the Nov pilot runs — highest
    future priority, per `INSTAGRAM_STRATEGY.md`'s note that social proof
    is the highest-converting content type once it exists)
    - Hook: guest's own words, on camera, unscripted
    - Value: their honest reaction to one moment of the trip
    - CTA: "Book the next departure — link in bio"

Reels 6, 8, and 9 need **no new footage** — they can be shot today on a
phone with zero coordination. Start there while sauce-day and artisan
visits get scheduled.

## 5. Posting calendar — first 4 weeks

Cadence follows `INSTAGRAM_STRATEGY.md` (2–3 posts/week, stories whenever
something's happening). Concrete week-by-week:

- **Week 1:** Launch posts from §2 (welcome → why-quote → FAQ carousel).
  Set the profile picture. Write the bio (see §6). Shoot Reels #6, #8 on
  phone — post one.
- **Week 2:** Shoot sauce day if the window's still open. Post Reel #1
  within 48 hours of shooting (recency matters for this kind of content).
  Post Reel #9 (Etruscan-not-Roman) as a carousel or Reel depending on what
  footage exists.
- **Week 3:** Visit Davide and/or Emiliano. Post Reels #3, #5 (people
  pillar — Rasna's hardest-to-copy content). Start daily-ish Stories from
  whatever's happening.
- **Week 4:** Visit the tombs/Via Clodia solo for place b-roll. Post Reel
  #2. Post the pricing/FAQ Reel #4 — this is the one that should carry the
  founding-guest discount urgency explicitly, since that offer exists to
  break a real 0/8-booked freeze (`BOOKING_STATUS.md`).

By end of week 4 that's roughly 3 launch graphics + 5 Reels + ongoing
Stories — a real account, not a placeholder, built entirely from footage
that required no new relationships to capture.

Account name: `@rasnaexperience` or `@rasna.experience` (match the domain,

`rasnaexperience.com` directly (single destination, no Linktree needed —
the site already has the booking flow, guide pages, and everything else in

**The selling mechanism, explicitly:** Instagram's job here isn't to close
the sale — the site with its live Stripe checkout does that. Instagram's
job is to make someone start wanting this before they've searched for it
(per the framing already in `INSTAGRAM_STRATEGY.md`'s opening). Every post
should end at one of two places: the guide pages (top-of-funnel, SEO
content getting a second life) or the booking section directly (bottom-of-
funnel, especially once the founding-guest discount is mentioned — scarcity
on a real, capacity-limited offer is honest urgency here, not manufactured).
